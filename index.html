Before you land

Handy basics we kept on one page so you do not have to dig around.

Topic What to know
Language English works almost everywhere. You will also hear Mandarin, Malay, and Tamil.
Currency Singapore dollar (SGD). Cards and mobile pay are common, but keep some cash for hawker stalls.
Getting around MRT and buses cover most tourist areas. Taxis and ride-hailing are easy for late nights.
Weather Hot and humid year-round. Pack an umbrella — short rain showers happen often.
