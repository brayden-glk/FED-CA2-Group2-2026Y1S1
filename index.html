Singapore's History

Singapore's history dates back to the 14th century as a trading port. In 1819, Sir Stamford Raffles established it as a British trading settlement, helping it grow into an important commercial hub. After gaining independence in 1965, Singapore transformed into one of the world's leading cities, known for its modern skyline, multicultural society, and thriving economy. Today, it is a vibrant destination where rich heritage meets innovation.

Why you should come

Singapore offers something for everyone. Visit iconic landmarks like Marina Bay Sands and Gardens by the Bay, enjoy delicious hawker food, and explore vibrant cultural districts such as Chinatown and Little India. With its clean, safe environment, efficient public transport, and mix of modern attractions and rich culture, Singapore is a memorable destination for every visitor.
